FlyManager User Guide

A comprehensive guide to using and managing your fly stocks with FlyManager

Getting Started

Welcome to FlyManager! This system helps you efficiently manage your Drosophila stock collection. This guide will help you navigate and use the application effectively.

System Overview

FlyManager is designed to help research labs maintain organized records of:

  • Genetic stocks with detailed genotype information
  • Crosses between stocks
  • Physical tray organization for your flies
  • Flipping schedules and reminders

System Requirements

FlyManager is a web-based application that works with most modern web browsers. For the best experience, we recommend:

  • Chrome (version 88 or newer)
  • Firefox (version 85 or newer)
  • Safari (version 14 or newer)
  • Edge (version 88 or newer)

Initial Setup

1 Admin Account Setup: The first time you run FlyManager, you'll need to create an admin account.

2 Configure Lab Settings: As an admin, navigate to Settings to configure your lab information.

3 Create User Accounts: Add accounts for lab members who will use the system.

4 Set Up Trays: Create virtual trays that match your physical lab organization.

5 Import Initial Data: If you have existing stock records, use the data import feature to populate the system.

Authentication

FlyManager uses a robust authentication system to ensure your data remains secure.

Login Process

1 Navigate to the FlyManager login page.

2 Select your username from the dropdown menu.

3 Enter your password and click "Login".

Forgot Password

If you forget your password:

1 Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

2 Select your username from the dropdown menu.

3 Enter the master password (available from your lab admin).

4 Create a new password and confirm it.

User Registration (if enabled)

If self-registration is enabled:

1 Click the "Register" link on the login page.

2 Fill out the registration form with your details.

3 Submit the form to create your account.

Dashboard

After logging in, you'll be directed to the homepage dashboard, which provides an overview of your fly management system.

Dashboard Sections

Quick Actions

The top section contains buttons for common actions:

  • Add New Stock - Create a new genetic stock record
  • Create Cross - Create a new cross between existing stocks
  • View Schedule - See your upcoming flip schedule
  • Add Tray - Create a new tray for organizing stocks

Overview Statistics

This section shows key metrics about your fly management system:

  • Total Stocks - Number of active genetic stocks
  • Total Crosses - Number of active crosses
  • Need Attention - Stocks/crosses requiring action
  • Tray Usage - Percentage of tray space currently utilized

Vial Statistics

Track your current vial counts:

  • Stock Vials - Total number of actively maintained stock vials
  • Cross Vials - Total number of actively maintained cross vials
  • Total Vials - Combined total of all vials

Urgent Items

This section highlights stocks and crosses that need immediate attention:

  • Overdue Items - Stocks/crosses past their scheduled flip date
  • Due Today - Stocks/crosses scheduled for flipping today

Today's Schedule

Shows the specific stocks and crosses that need to be flipped today.

Upcoming Schedule

Provides a preview of flips scheduled for the next 7 days.

Recent Activity

Lists recent actions performed in the system, such as flips, edits, or new additions.

Stock Management

The Stock Management system helps you catalog, organize, and maintain your genetic stocks.

Stock Explorer

The Stock Explorer provides a comprehensive view of all your stocks with powerful filtering options.

1 Access the Stock Explorer from the main menu or by clicking "Stock Explorer" in the dropdown.

2 Use the filters at the top to narrow down stocks by type, tray, status, etc.

3 Click the search box to find stocks by name, genotype, or other attributes.

4 Select stocks by clicking the checkbox in each stock card.

Adding a New Stock

To add a new genetic stock to your collection:

1 Click "Add New Stock" from the dashboard or Stock Explorer.

2 Fill out the stock details form, including:

  • Source ID: External reference number (e.g., Bloomington Stock Center number)
  • Name: A descriptive name for the stock
  • Genotype: The genetic composition, split by chromosome
  • Type: The type of stock (e.g., wildtype, mutant)
  • Tray Location: Where the stock is physically stored
  • Maintenance Parameters: Vial lifetime, flip frequency, etc.

3 Click "Add Stock" to save the new entry.

Tip: Auto-population

If adding a stock from Bloomington, enter the BDSC number in the Source ID field and click the "Fetch" button to automatically populate genotype information.

Viewing and Editing Stock Details

1 From the Stock Explorer, click the External Link icon on any stock.

2 The stock details page shows all information about the stock.

3 Click the "Edit" button to modify any stock details.

4 After making changes, click "Save Changes" to update the stock.

Flipping Stocks

Regular flipping (transferring flies to fresh food) is essential for maintaining healthy stocks.

1 Access the Flipper interface from the main menu.

2 Scan the vial barcode or enter the stock ID manually.

3 Update the status if needed (Healthy, Showing Issues, Needs Refresh).

4 Add any relevant comments about the flip.

5 Click "Flip" to record the flip and update the schedule.

Cross Management

The Cross Management system helps you track genetic crosses between your stocks.

Cross Explorer

Similar to the Stock Explorer, the Cross Explorer provides a view of all your crosses with filtering options.

1 Access the Cross Explorer from the main menu.

2 Use filters to find crosses by male/female genotype, tray, or status.

3 Select crosses using the checkboxes for bulk operations.

Creating a New Cross

1 Click "Create Cross" from the dashboard or Cross Explorer.

2 Select the male and female stocks from your existing stocks.

3 Fill out cross details like name, tray location, and maintenance parameters.

4 Review the predicted offspring genotype (automatically calculated).

5 Click "Create Cross" to save the new cross.

Tip: Genotype Prediction

The system will automatically predict the offspring genotype based on the male and female stocks. You can review and edit this prediction before saving.

Managing Crosses

The lifecycle of a cross typically involves:

  1. Creating the cross between male and female stocks
  2. Regular flipping to maintain the cross
  3. Collecting virgin females when needed
  4. Potentially converting successful crosses into new stocks

Converting a Cross to a Stock

1 View the cross details from the Cross Explorer.

2 Click the "Convert to Stock" button.

3 Review and confirm the new stock details.

4 Click "Create Stock" to create a new stock from this cross.

Tray Management

The Tray Management system helps you organize your physical fly storage efficiently.

Viewing Trays

1 Access the Tray Management module from the main menu.

2 View a list of all your current trays.

3 Click on any tray to view its detailed layout.

Adding a New Tray

1 Click "Add Tray" from the dashboard or Tray Management page.

2 Enter a unique Tray ID.

3 Specify the number of rows and columns in the tray.

4 Add any notes about the tray.

5 Click "Create Tray" to add it to the system.

Tray Visualization

When viewing a tray, you'll see a grid representation of the physical tray:

  • Empty positions are shown in a neutral color
  • Occupied positions show the stock/cross name and ID
  • Blocked positions (due to multi-vial stocks/crosses) are indicated

Managing Items in Trays

1 To add a stock or cross to a tray, open the tray view.

2 Click the "Add Item" button.

3 Select a stock or cross from the list.

4 Drag and drop the item to the desired position in the tray.

5 Click "Save" to confirm the placement.

Understanding Tray Blocking

Some stocks or crosses require multiple vial positions. When you place such an item in a tray, the system will automatically block the appropriate number of adjacent positions.

Rearranging Tray Items

1 In tray view, drag a stock/cross to a new location.

2 The system will check if the new location has sufficient space.

3 Release to place the item in the new position.

4 Click "Save Changes" to update the tray arrangement.

Flip Schedule

The Flip Schedule helps you maintain your stocks and crosses on a regular basis.

Viewing the Schedule

1 Access the Flip Schedule from the main menu.

2 View a calendar-style display of upcoming flips.

3 Each day shows the stocks and crosses that need attention.

Flip Interface

The Flip Interface is used to record when you've flipped a stock or cross:

1 Access the Flipper from the main menu.

2 Enter a stock/cross ID or scan the barcode.

3 Update the status and add any comments.

4 Click "Flip" to record the maintenance action.

Understanding Flip Parameters

Each stock and cross has specific maintenance parameters:

  • Vial Lifetime: How long a vial can be used (in days)
  • Flip Frequency: How often the stock should be flipped (in days)
  • Developmental Time: Time from egg to eclosion (in days)

These parameters determine when items appear on your flip schedule.

Email Reminders

If configured, the system can send email reminders about upcoming flips:

1 Daily emails summarize items that need flipping that day.

2 Click links in the email to view details in FlyManager.

Bulk Operations

FlyManager allows you to perform actions on multiple stocks or crosses simultaneously.

Using the Cart System

1 In Stock or Cross Explorer, select items using checkboxes.

2 Click "Add to Cart" to add selected items to your cart.

3 The cart icon shows how many items are currently selected.

4 Click the cart to show/hide the cart panel.

Bulk Flipping

1 Add stocks/crosses to your cart.

2 Click "Bulk Flip" in the cart panel.

3 Set the status and add any comments that apply to all items.

4 Click "Confirm Flip" to process all items at once.

Bulk Status Changes

1 Add stocks/crosses to your cart.

2 Click "Change Status" and select the new status.

3 Confirm the change to update all selected items.

Bulk Tray Operations

1 Add stocks/crosses to your cart.

2 Click "Remove from Tray" to clear tray positions for all selected items.

Data Import/Export

FlyManager allows you to import and export data in bulk for easier management.

Exporting Data

1 From the main menu, select "Download Data".

2 Your browser will download an Excel file containing all your data.

3 The file includes separate sheets for stocks, crosses, and trays.

Importing Data

To import data from an Excel file:

1 From the main menu, select "Upload Data".

2 Click "Choose File" and select your Excel file.

3 Select the import options (update existing records, add new records).

4 Click "Upload" to process the file.

Important Note on Data Import

When importing data:

  • The Excel file must follow the correct format. Download a current export first as a template.
  • Be careful when updating existing records, as this will overwrite current data.
  • UniqueID fields must match exactly for updates to work correctly.

Label Generation

FlyManager can generate printable labels for your vials.

Generating Individual Labels

1 Add stocks/crosses to your cart in the Explorer.

2 Click "Generate Labels" in the cart panel.

3 Specify how many blank spaces to include (if any).

4 The system will generate a PDF file with labels.

5 Print the PDF on label paper.

Generating Labels from Schedule

1 Go to the Flip Schedule.

2 For any day, click the "Generate Labels" button.

3 Specify blank spaces as needed.

4 The system generates labels for all items scheduled for that day.

Label Format

Labels include:

  • Stock/Cross name
  • Unique ID
  • Genotype or cross information
  • Date flipped
  • User initials
  • QR code for scanning

Scanner Usage

FlyManager supports barcode/QR code scanners to streamline the flip process.

Setting Up a Scanner

1 Connect your barcode scanner to your computer via USB.

2 Go to the Flip Interface.

3 Select your scanner from the dropdown menu.

4 Click "Start Scanning" to begin the scanning session.

Scanning Process

1 With scanning active, scan the QR code on a vial label.

2 The system will automatically load the stock/cross details.

3 Update the status and add any comments.

4 Click "Flip" or scan another vial to continue.

Accessing Admin Settings

Lab Information

System Appearance

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I determine how many vials a stock needs?

The number of vials a stock needs is calculated based on its maintenance parameters:

For a given stock, the system calculates: Vial Lifetime / Flip Frequency (rounded up)

This ensures you always have enough vials to maintain the stock across its entire lifecycle.

What do the different status colors mean?

The color indicators represent different status levels:

  • Green (Healthy): Stock/cross is doing well
  • Yellow (Showing Issues): Minor issues observed
  • Red (Needs Refresh): Immediate attention required
  • Blue (Ordered): Stock has been ordered but not received
  • Grey (No longer maintained): Stock is archived
Why can't I place a stock in my tray?

There are several potential reasons:

  1. The position is already occupied by another stock or cross
  2. The position is blocked by adjacent multi-vial items
  3. There isn't enough contiguous space for the vials required
  4. The stock has status "No longer maintained"

Try choosing a different position with adequate space.

How is tray usage percentage calculated?

Tray usage is calculated as:

(Occupied Positions / Total Tray Positions) × 100%

Occupied positions include both positions with items and positions blocked by multi-vial items.

How do I recover a deleted stock or cross?

Instead of deleting stocks or crosses, it's recommended to change their status to "No longer maintained" to preserve historical data.

If you accidentally deleted a stock or cross:

  1. Check if you have a recent data export containing the item
  2. If available, use the data import feature to restore it
  3. If not, you'll need to recreate the stock/cross manually
What's the difference between vial lifetime and flip frequency?

Vial Lifetime is how long a single vial remains viable before it should be discarded (typically 14-21 days).

Flip Frequency is how often you should transfer flies to new food (typically 7 days).

For example, with a vial lifetime of 14 days and a flip frequency of 7 days, you would:

  • Day 0: Start vial 1
  • Day 7: Flip to vial 2
  • Day 14: Flip to vial 1 (after discarding the old vial 1)

Glossary

Stock
A genetically stable fly line maintained in the lab.
Cross
A mating between two different stocks to produce offspring with a specific genotype.
Flip
The process of transferring flies to fresh food to maintain a healthy population.
Tray
A physical container holding multiple vials of flies.
Vial
A single container of flies with food medium.
Vial Lifetime
The time period a vial can be used before it should be discarded.
Flip Frequency
How often flies should be transferred to fresh food.
Developmental Time
The time required for development from egg to adult fly.
Virgin Collection
The process of collecting newly emerged, unmated female flies.
Genotype
The genetic makeup of a fly, often described by chromosome.
UniqueID
A system-generated identifier for each stock or cross.
Source ID
An external reference number, such as a Bloomington Stock Center number.
BDSC
Bloomington Drosophila Stock Center, a common source for fly stocks.
